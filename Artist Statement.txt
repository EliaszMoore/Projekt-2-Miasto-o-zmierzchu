Miasto o zmierzchu -- City at Dusk
This project started from a mixture of ideas. I knew at the start I wanted to randomise architecture, but I was indecisive on what to do. I initially wanted to make an infinite staircase similar to the Penrose steps. However, I abandoned this idea in favour of randomly repeating buildings. There are 2 different buildings, they are random in amount on each generation. Sometimes you get more of building 1, sometimes more of building 2. Their locations are random within the sketch, and they have been allowed to overlap, allowing for crazy clusters. The windows on each building type are set, but may turn on and of randomly every 2 seconds. I stress the "may", sometimes they will not turn off or will not turn on, this can be controlled in the code to set the likelihood of being on or off.
